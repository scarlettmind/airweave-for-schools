For Heads of Boarding

Why the bed is a pastoral‑care decision

01

Teenagers sleep less than they need

Adolescents need 8–10 hours. Boarding schools are the only institutions that fully control the environment in which those hours happen.

02

One bed, many bodies

Dormitory beds serve a new student every year. Adjustable, washable, breathable surfaces adapt to growing bodies and meet hygiene standards.

03

Parents ask about wellbeing

Families choosing a world‑class school now ask how it cares for the whole child. Sleep is a visible, measurable answer.
